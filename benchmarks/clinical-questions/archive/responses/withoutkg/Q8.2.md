### **Statistical Significance:**
The data shows that **carbapenem therapy is associated with a higher 60-day UTI recurrence rate** compared to narrow-spectrum agents:
- **Relative risk increase**: ~54% higher recurrence rate with carbapenems

- **Narrow-spectrum**: Average 8.5 days to recurrence

- **Males**: Carbapenems 20.54% vs Narrow-spectrum 20.26%

## **Clinical Interpretation:**

### **Potential Explanations for Higher Carbapenem Recurrence:**

1. **Selection Bias**: Patients receiving carbapenems likely had more severe infections or resistant organisms
2. **Microbiome Disruption**: Broad-spectrum carbapenems may cause greater disruption to protective urogenital flora
3. **Resistance Development**: Carbapenem use may select for resistant organisms leading to treatment failures
4. **Severity Confounding**: Sicker patients requiring carbapenems may have underlying conditions predisposing to recurrence

### **Clinical Implications:**

**The data suggests that narrow-spectrum agents are associated with lower 60-day UTI recurrence rates compared to carbapenems.** However, several important caveats apply:

1. **Indication Bias**: Carbapenems are typically reserved for severe or resistant infections
2. **Case Mix**: Patients requiring carbapenems likely differ systematically from those receiving narrow-spectrum agents
3. **Causation vs Correlation**: The higher recurrence rate may reflect patient characteristics rather than treatment effect

## **Recommendations:**

1. **First-line therapy**: Continue using narrow-spectrum agents for uncomplicated gram-negative UTIs
2. **Carbapenem stewardship**: Reserve carbapenems for documented resistant organisms or severe infections
3. **Further research**: Prospective studies controlling for infection severity and organism resistance are needed
4. **Individual assessment**: Consider patient-specific factors including previous cultures and resistance patterns

- Observational study design limits causal inference

- Different indications for carbapenem vs narrow-spectrum therapy
