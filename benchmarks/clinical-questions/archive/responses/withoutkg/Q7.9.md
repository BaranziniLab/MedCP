## **SQL Query Used:**
```sql
SELECT TOP 20
    c_drug.concept_name as medication,
    c_drug.concept_id as drug_concept_id,
    COUNT(DISTINCT de.person_id) as unique_patients,
    COUNT(*) as total_exposures
FROM omop.condition_occurrence co
INNER JOIN omop.drug_exposure de ON co.person_id = de.person_id 
INNER JOIN omop.concept c_drug ON de.drug_concept_id = c_drug.concept_id
WHERE co.condition_concept_id IN (40480225, 44821835, 44822997, 44829951, 438409, 4149904, 45765796, 4253962, 44784525, 4149353)
  AND de.drug_exposure_start_date >= co.condition_start_date
  AND de.drug_exposure_start_date <= DATEADD(year, 1, co.condition_start_date)
  AND (c_drug.concept_name LIKE '%methylphenidate%' 
       OR c_drug.concept_name LIKE '%atomoxetine%'
       OR c_drug.concept_name LIKE '%lisdexamfetamine%'
       OR c_drug.concept_name LIKE '%dextroamphetamine%'
       OR c_drug.concept_name LIKE '%amphetamine%'
       OR c_drug.concept_name LIKE '%guanfacine%'
       OR c_drug.concept_name LIKE '%clonidine%')
GROUP BY c_drug.concept_name, c_drug.concept_id
ORDER BY total_exposures DESC
```
