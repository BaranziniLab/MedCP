### 1. **Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System (RAAS)**
**Links**: Hypertension ↔ CKD ↔ Heart Failure ↔ Diabetes
- **Mechanism**: Chronic activation leads to vasoconstriction, sodium retention, and tissue remodeling
- **Effects**: Directly contributes to hypertension, accelerates kidney damage, promotes cardiac remodeling, and impairs insulin sensitivity

### 2. **Insulin Resistance and Metabolic Syndrome**
**Links**: Hypertension ↔ Diabetes ↔ Obesity ↔ Hyperlipidemia
- **Mechanism**: Insulin resistance creates a cascade of metabolic dysfunction
- **Effects**: 
  - Promotes sodium retention and sympathetic activation (hypertension)
  - Leads to hyperglycemia (diabetes)
  - Increases lipogenesis (obesity)
  - Alters lipid metabolism (dyslipidemia)

### 3. **Chronic Inflammation**
**Links**: All major comorbidities share inflammatory pathways
- **Mechanism**: Elevated cytokines (IL-6, TNF-α, CRP) create systemic inflammation
- **Effects**:
  - Endothelial dysfunction (hypertension, CAD)
  - Insulin resistance (diabetes)
  - Adipose tissue inflammation (obesity)
  - Glomerular inflammation (CKD)

### 4. **Oxidative Stress**
**Links**: Hypertension ↔ Diabetes ↔ CKD ↔ Atherosclerosis
- **Mechanism**: Increased reactive oxygen species (ROS) production
- **Effects**: Damages blood vessels, kidneys, and promotes atherosclerosis

### 5. **Sympathetic Nervous System Activation**
**Links**: Hypertension ↔ Obesity ↔ Heart Failure ↔ CKD
- **Mechanism**: Chronic stress response and neurohormonal activation
- **Effects**: Increases heart rate, vasoconstriction, and promotes arrhythmias

### 6. **Endothelial Dysfunction**
**Links**: Central to cardiovascular complications
- **Mechanism**: Reduced nitric oxide availability and increased vasoconstriction
- **Effects**: Directly contributes to hypertension, atherosclerosis, and thrombosis

## Clinical Implications

### Metabolic Cluster (Most Common):

### Cardiorenal Cluster:
