**Main Query:**
```sql
SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT p.person_id) as children_with_asthma
FROM omop.person p
INNER JOIN omop.condition_occurrence co ON p.person_id = co.person_id
INNER JOIN omop.concept c ON co.condition_concept_id = c.concept_id
WHERE DATEDIFF(year, p.birth_datetime, co.condition_start_date) < 18
AND (c.concept_id = 317009 OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%asthma%')
AND c.domain_id = 'Condition'
AND c.standard_concept = 'S'
```
