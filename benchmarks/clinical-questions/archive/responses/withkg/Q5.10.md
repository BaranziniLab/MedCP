Based on my analysis of the clinical records data, here are the most frequent nutritional deficiency diagnoses among elderly patients (aged 65+):

## **SQL Query Used:**
```sql
SELECT 
    c.concept_name,
    COUNT(*) as diagnosis_count,
    COUNT(DISTINCT co.person_id) as unique_patients
FROM omop.condition_occurrence co
JOIN omop.person p ON co.person_id = p.person_id
JOIN omop.concept c ON co.condition_concept_id = c.concept_id
WHERE 
    YEAR(co.condition_start_date) - p.year_of_birth >= 65
    AND (
        LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%deficiency%' 
        OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%malnutrition%'
        OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%vitamin%defic%'
        OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%mineral%defic%'
        OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%nutritional%'
        OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%anemia%'
        OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%scurvy%'
        OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%rickets%'
        OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%pellagra%'
        OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%beriberi%'
    )
    AND c.standard_concept = 'S'
    AND c.invalid_reason IS NULL
GROUP BY c.concept_name, c.concept_id
HAVING COUNT(DISTINCT co.person_id) >= 5
ORDER BY diagnosis_count DESC;
```
