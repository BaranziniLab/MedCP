### **SQL Query Used:**
```sql
WITH lupus_patients AS (
    SELECT DISTINCT co.person_id
    FROM omop.condition_occurrence co
    JOIN omop.concept c ON co.condition_concept_id = c.concept_id
    WHERE LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%lupus%' 
       OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%systemic lupus erythematosus%'
       OR c.concept_name = 'SLE glomerulonephritis syndrome'
)
SELECT TOP 50
    mc.concept_name as measurement_name,
    COUNT(*) as test_frequency,
    COUNT(DISTINCT m.person_id) as patients_tested
FROM omop.measurement m
JOIN omop.concept mc ON m.measurement_concept_id = mc.concept_id
JOIN lupus_patients lp ON m.person_id = lp.person_id
WHERE mc.concept_name IS NOT NULL 
  AND mc.concept_name != ''
  AND (
      LOWER(mc.concept_name) LIKE '%ana%'
      OR LOWER(mc.concept_name) LIKE '%dsdna%'
      OR LOWER(mc.concept_name) LIKE '%complement%'
      OR LOWER(mc.concept_name) LIKE '%c3%'
      OR LOWER(mc.concept_name) LIKE '%c4%'
      OR LOWER(mc.concept_name) LIKE '%smith%'
      OR LOWER(mc.concept_name) LIKE '%anticardiolipin%'
      OR LOWER(mc.concept_name) LIKE '%anticoagulant%'
      OR LOWER(mc.concept_name) LIKE '%crp%'
      OR LOWER(mc.concept_name) LIKE '%esr%'
  )
GROUP BY mc.concept_name
ORDER BY test_frequency DESC
```

## **Most Frequent Lupus-Associated Lab Tests:**

### **1. Complement Tests (Most Frequent)**
- **Complement C4 [Mass/volume] in Serum or Plasma**: 32,324 tests, 4,703 patients
  - **Protein Measured**: Complement C4-A and C4-B proteins (CO4A_HUMAN, CO4B_HUMAN)
  - **Function**: Part of classical complement pathway; decreased levels indicate complement consumption due to immune complex formation

- **Complement C3 [Mass/volume] in Serum or Plasma**: 40,063 tests, 4,876 patients  
  - **Protein Measured**: Complement C3 protein (CO3_HUMAN)
  - **Function**: Central component of complement system; low levels suggest active disease

### **2. Autoantibody Tests**
- **DNA double strand IgG Ab [Units/volume] in Serum**: 30,514 tests, 4,623 patients
  - **Target**: Double-stranded DNA (dsDNA)
  - **Significance**: Highly specific for SLE; correlates with disease activity and renal involvement

- **Smith extractable nuclear Ab [Presence] in Serum**: 3,888 tests, 2,870 patients
  - **Target**: Smith (Sm) antigens - core proteins of small nuclear ribonucleoproteins (snRNPs)
  - **Proteins**: SNRPD1, SNRPD2, SNRPD3, SNRPE, SNRPF, SNRPG
  - **Significance**: Highly specific for SLE but less sensitive than anti-dsDNA

### **3. Antiphospholipid Antibodies**
- **Cardiolipin IgG Ab [Units/volume] in Serum**: 4,605 tests, 2,731 patients
- **Cardiolipin IgM Ab [Units/volume] in Serum**: 4,401 tests, 2,643 patients
  - **Target**: Cardiolipin (a phospholipid)
  - **Associated with**: Antiphospholipid syndrome, increased thrombosis risk

- **Lupus anticoagulant assay**: 3,803 tests, 1,585 patients
  - **Target**: Phospholipid-protein complexes
  - **Function**: Paradoxically increases clotting risk despite prolonging coagulation tests

### **4. Sjögren's Syndrome-Associated Antibodies**
- **Sjogrens syndrome-B extractable nuclear IgG Ab**: 3,440 tests, 2,618 patients
  - **Target**: La/SSB protein (TROVE2_HUMAN)
  - **Function**: RNA-binding protein involved in RNA processing

- **Sjogrens syndrome-A extractable nuclear 52kD Ab**: 1,248 tests, 1,021 patients
  - **Target**: Ro52 protein (TRIM21_HUMAN)
  - **Function**: E3 ubiquitin ligase involved in innate immunity

### **5. Inflammatory Markers**
- **C reactive protein [Mass/volume] in Serum or Plasma**: 38,898 tests, 4,742 patients
  - **Protein**: C-reactive protein (CRP_HUMAN)
  - **Function**: Acute-phase protein indicating inflammation

- **Erythrocyte sedimentation rate**: 68,443 tests, 6,264 patients
  - **Measures**: Rate of red blood cell settling (affected by inflammatory proteins like fibrinogen)

### **6. Additional Specific Tests**
- **Anti-beta2 glycoprotein 1 antibody level**: 3,617 tests, 1,497 patients
  - **Target**: Beta-2 glycoprotein I
  - **Associated with**: Antiphospholipid syndrome

## **Key Insights:**

1. **Complement proteins** (C3, C4) are the most frequently tested, reflecting their importance in monitoring lupus activity
2. **Anti-dsDNA antibodies** are highly specific and correlate with nephritis
3. **Antiphospholipid antibodies** are crucial for assessing thrombotic risk
4. **RNA-processing proteins** (Sm, La/SSB, Ro52) are common autoimmune targets
5. **Inflammatory markers** help assess overall disease activity
