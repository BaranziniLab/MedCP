### Key Findings:

**SQL Queries Used:**
1. **Main Query:**
```sql
SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT vo.visit_occurrence_id) as inpatient_encounters_with_pneumonia
FROM omop.visit_occurrence vo
JOIN omop.condition_occurrence co ON vo.visit_occurrence_id = co.visit_occurrence_id
JOIN omop.concept c ON co.condition_concept_id = c.concept_id
WHERE vo.visit_concept_id IN (8717, 9201) -- Inpatient Hospital and Inpatient Visit
AND LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%pneumonia%'
AND c.standard_concept = 'S'
AND c.domain_id = 'Condition'
AND vo.visit_start_date >= DATEADD(YEAR, -1, GETDATE());
```
