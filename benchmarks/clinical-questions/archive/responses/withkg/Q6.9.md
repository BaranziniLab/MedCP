## **Clinical Findings: Most Common Mental Health Co-Occurrences with Pediatric Obesity**

**SQL Query Used:**
```sql
WITH pediatric_patients AS (
  SELECT DISTINCT p.person_id, p.year_of_birth, p.gender_concept_id
  FROM omop.person p
  WHERE p.year_of_birth >= 2000  -- Children and adolescents
),
obesity_pediatric AS (
  SELECT DISTINCT co.person_id, co.condition_start_date,
         YEAR(co.condition_start_date) - pp.year_of_birth as age_at_diagnosis
  FROM omop.condition_occurrence co
  JOIN omop.concept c ON co.condition_concept_id = c.concept_id
  JOIN pediatric_patients pp ON co.person_id = pp.person_id
  WHERE (c.concept_id IN (4060985, 4171972, 4087487, 4079899, 4235799) -- obesity concepts
         OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%child%obesit%'
         OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%pediatric%obesit%')
    AND c.standard_concept = 'S'
    AND c.invalid_reason IS NULL
    AND YEAR(co.condition_start_date) - pp.year_of_birth <= 18
),
mental_health_pediatric AS (
  SELECT co.person_id, c.concept_id, c.concept_name,
         YEAR(co.condition_start_date) - pp.year_of_birth as age_at_diagnosis,
         co.condition_start_date
  FROM omop.condition_occurrence co
  JOIN omop.concept c ON co.condition_concept_id = c.concept_id  
  JOIN pediatric_patients pp ON co.person_id = pp.person_id
  WHERE (LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%depress%' 
         OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%anxiety%'
         OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%adhd%'
         OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%attention deficit%'
         OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%conduct%disorder%'
         OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%bipolar%'
         OR LOWER(c.concept_name) LIKE '%eating%disorder%')
    AND c.domain_id = 'Condition'
    AND c.standard_concept = 'S'
    AND c.invalid_reason IS NULL
    AND YEAR(co.condition_start_date) - pp.year_of_birth <= 18
)
SELECT TOP 15
  mh.concept_name,
  COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN op.person_id IS NOT NULL THEN mh.person_id END) as with_obesity,
  COUNT(DISTINCT mh.person_id) as total_mental_health,
  CAST(COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN op.person_id IS NOT NULL THEN mh.person_id END) AS FLOAT) 
    / COUNT(DISTINCT mh.person_id) * 100 as co_occurrence_percentage,
  AVG(CAST(mh.age_at_diagnosis AS FLOAT)) as avg_age_at_diagnosis
FROM mental_health_pediatric mh
LEFT JOIN obesity_pediatric op ON mh.person_id = op.person_id
GROUP BY mh.concept_name
HAVING COUNT(DISTINCT mh.person_id) >= 10
ORDER BY with_obesity DESC
```

### **Key Appetite/Stress Pathways:**

**1. Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Adrenal (HPA) Axis:**
- **NR3C1** (glucocorticoid receptor): Links obesity, depression, and anxiety
- **CRH** (corticotropin-releasing hormone): Associated with depression and anxiety
- **CRHR1** (CRH receptor): Connected to anxiety disorders
- Chronic stress → cortisol dysregulation → weight gain and mood disorders

**2. Leptin Signaling Pathway:**
- **LEP** (leptin): Associated with obesity and morbid obesity
- **LEPR** (leptin receptor): Key in appetite regulation
- Leptin resistance in obesity → impaired satiety signaling
- Leptin also affects mood regulation through brain reward circuits

**3. Serotonin System:**
- **HTR2A** (serotonin 2A receptor): Linked to anxiety disorders
- **SLC6A4** (serotonin transporter): Associated with depression and anxiety
- Serotonin regulates both appetite and mood
- Disrupted serotonin → emotional eating and depression/anxiety

**4. Hypothalamic Appetite Control:**
- **POMC** (pro-opiomelanocortin): Associated with both obesity and anxiety
- **NPY** (neuropeptide Y): Links obesity and anxiety
- **AGRP** (agouti-related peptide): Connected to obesity
- **MC4R** (melanocortin 4 receptor): Critical for appetite regulation

**5. Neurotrophic Factors:**
- **BDNF** (brain-derived neurotrophic factor): Associated with obesity, depression, and anxiety
- BDNF supports neuroplasticity and mood regulation
- Reduced BDNF → both cognitive issues and metabolic dysfunction

**6. Appetite Hormone Dysregulation:**
- **GHRL** (ghrelin): The "hunger hormone" linked to obesity
- Ghrelin also affects mood and stress response
- Dysregulated ghrelin → both overeating and mood instability

## **Mechanistic Links:**

1. **Chronic Inflammation:** Obesity creates inflammatory states that affect brain function and mood
2. **Reward System Dysfunction:** Shared dopaminergic pathways in food addiction and mood regulation
3. **Sleep Disruption:** Obesity-related sleep disorders worsen mental health
4. **Social/Psychological Stress:** Weight stigma creates additional stress, worsening both conditions
5. **Medication Effects:** Some psychiatric medications can cause weight gain

## **Clinical Implications:**

- **Integrated Treatment Approach:** Address both obesity and mental health simultaneously
- **Early Intervention:** Given the high co-occurrence rates (8-15%), screen for mental health in obese children
- **Lifestyle Interventions:** Exercise and nutrition programs that benefit both conditions
- **Family-Based Treatment:** Address household stress and eating patterns
