## Neurochemical Explanation for Fall Risk Differences

### Why Anticholinergic Deprescribing May Have Lower Fall Risk:

1. **Central Cholinergic Function Recovery:**
   - Anticholinergics block muscarinic receptors in the brain
   - Withdrawal allows restoration of cholinergic neurotransmission
   - Improved cognitive function and attention (critical for fall prevention)
   - Enhanced vestibular processing and spatial orientation

2. **Peripheral Effects:**
   - Reduced anticholinergic burden improves orthostatic stability
   - Better autonomic regulation of blood pressure
   - Improved muscle strength and coordination

### Why Sedative-Hypnotic Deprescribing May Have Higher Fall Risk:

1. **GABA System Withdrawal:**
   - Rebound hyperexcitability of GABA-A receptors
   - Increased anxiety and sleep disruption
   - Potential withdrawal syndrome with tremor and instability

2. **Sleep Architecture Disruption:**
   - Rebound insomnia affects daytime alertness
   - Increased fatigue and reduced reaction times
   - Poor sleep quality affects balance and coordination
