   - Reduces macrophage activation and neutrophil recruitment

   - Bronchodilation through cAMP-mediated smooth muscle relaxation
   - Limited anti-inflammatory effects
   - Primarily addresses airway obstruction rather than inflammation

2. **Muscarinic Receptor Antagonism (CHRM3)**:
   - Reduces acetylcholine-mediated bronchoconstriction
   - Decreases mucus secretion
   - Minimal impact on underlying inflammatory processes
