## Neurotransmitter/Circuit Mechanisms:

### **Ketamine-Based Mechanisms:**

1. **Primary Target - NMDA Receptor Antagonism:**
   - Blocks NMDA glutamate receptors (GluN1, GluN2A, GluN2B subunits)
   - Rapid antidepressant effects through glutamatergic pathway modulation
   - Promotes synaptic plasticity and neurogenesis

2. **Secondary Mechanisms:**
   - **cAMP-PDE4 pathway:** Binds to PDE4A/B/C/D enzymes, potentially enhancing cAMP signaling
   - **Voltage-gated sodium channels:** Affects Nav1.4 channels
   - **AMPA receptor potentiation:** Downstream effect enhancing excitatory neurotransmission

3. **Neuroplasticity Enhancement:**
   - Increases BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor)
   - Promotes dendritic spine formation
   - Rapid synaptogenesis in prefrontal cortex

### **ECT Mechanisms:**

1. **Multi-neurotransmitter System Effects:**
   - **Serotonin system:** Increases 5-HT receptor sensitivity and serotonin availability
   - **Dopamine system:** Modulates dopaminergic pathways in limbic regions
   - **GABA system:** Enhances GABAergic inhibition post-seizure
   - **Norepinephrine system:** Increases noradrenergic neurotransmission

2. **Seizure-Induced Neuroplasticity:**
   - Massive neuronal depolarization and synchronization
   - Increases multiple neurotrophic factors (BDNF, GDNF, VEGF)
   - Promotes hippocampal neurogenesis
   - Modulates HPA axis activity

3. **Circuit-Level Changes:**
   - Alters default mode network connectivity
   - Reduces hyperconnectivity in limbic-cortical circuits
   - Normalizes prefrontal-limbic dysregulation

- **Rapid onset** (hours to days vs weeks for ECT)

### **ECT Advantages:**
- **Broader neurotransmitter impact** (serotonin, dopamine, GABA, norepinephrine)
