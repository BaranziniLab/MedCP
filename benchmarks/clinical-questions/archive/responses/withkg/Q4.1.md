   - Q3 2020 vs Q3 2019: Increase (+4.5%)
   - Q4 2020 vs Q4 2019: Increase (+14.9%)

### **Clinical Interpretation:**

Contrary to what might be expected during the COVID-19 pandemic, systemic immunosuppressant prescriptions actually **increased** in 2020. This could be due to:

1. **Delayed care catch-up**: Patients may have received multiple prescriptions to make up for delayed care in early 2020
2. **Stockpiling behavior**: Patients and providers may have prescribed larger quantities due to pandemic uncertainties
3. **Increased monitoring**: More frequent prescriptions for closer patient monitoring
4. **Telehealth expansion**: Better access to care through telemedicine

### **Limitations:**

- External factors like healthcare policy changes, supply chain issues, or reporting changes could affect the data
