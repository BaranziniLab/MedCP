- **CDI diagnoses:** monthly CDI condition eras were fairly stable through 2024, mostly ~80–130/month, with peaks around **Aug–Sep 2023** and **Dec 2024**, then declined sharply through 2025 to **13 by Nov 2025**.
- **Inpatient broad-spectrum antibiotic starts:** rose into 2024, peaking around **Jul–Dec 2024** (~19k–21k starts/month), then also declined through 2025.
- **Co-occurrence:** months with higher CDI generally coincided with higher inpatient broad-spectrum antibiotic starts; the month-level Pearson correlation was approximately **r = 0.86** across complete months.
- **Caveat:** this is an ecological, unadjusted same-month association. It is likely influenced by shared utilization/data-completeness trends, especially the 2025 decline, and does **not** prove patient-level antibiotic exposure caused CDI.
