Monthly first-observed depression diagnoses were fairly steady but slightly declined over 2019–2021.

Key trends:
- 2019 was highest overall: mostly ~1,200–1,350 new diagnoses/month.
- 2020 dipped early, especially Jan–Apr (~1,113–1,159), then rebounded mid/late year to ~1,200–1,272/month.

- Peaks:

  - Jan 2019: 1,308
  - Jul/Oct 2020: 1,263/1,272
- Overall pattern: no sharp sustained pandemic-era spike; instead, a modest downward drift from 2019 into 2021.
