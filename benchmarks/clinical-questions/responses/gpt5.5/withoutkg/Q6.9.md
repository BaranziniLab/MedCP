Likely appetite/stress pathways linking these conditions with obesity:

- **Anxiety / stress disorders:** chronic activation of the HPA axis → cortisol dysregulation, sleep disruption, emotional eating, preference for energy-dense foods.
- **ADHD:** impulsivity, reward sensitivity, irregular routines, sleep disturbance, and sometimes appetite effects from stimulant or non-stimulant treatment.
- **Autism spectrum disorders:** restricted food preferences, sensory-driven diets, lower activity in some children, sleep problems, and medication exposure such as atypical antipsychotics.
- **Depression / adjustment disorders:** reduced activity, disrupted sleep, emotional eating, altered leptin/ghrelin signaling, and inflammation-related metabolic effects.
- **Binge eating:** direct behavioral pathway through loss-of-control eating and reward-circuit dysregulation.
- **Bipolar disorders:** obesity risk may be strongly medication-mediated, especially via atypical antipsychotics and mood stabilizers, plus circadian disruption.

Caveat: these are EHR co-diagnosis frequencies, not causal estimates. Diagnosis coding, care-seeking patterns, medication exposure, and underdiagnosis can strongly affect the observed rates.
