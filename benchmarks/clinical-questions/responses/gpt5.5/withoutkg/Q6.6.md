Interpretation: the dominant extraintestinal signal is musculoskeletal inflammation, especially broad arthritis/spondyloarthritis. A plausible shared mechanism is gut–joint immune crosstalk driven by Th17/IL-23 signaling, TNF-α, IL-6, and innate myeloid activation. Barrier dysfunction and microbial antigen exposure in IBD can prime mucosal T cells that traffic to joints, skin, and eye via shared adhesion/chemokine programs.

Other patterns fit related immune axes:

- **Psoriasis / psoriatic arthritis**: IL-23–Th17–IL-17 pathway overlap with Crohn’s/UC biology.
- **Uveitis / iritis / episcleritis**: systemic Th1/Th17 inflammation and HLA-associated mucosal–ocular immune activation.
- **Pyoderma gangrenosum / erythema nodosum**: neutrophilic and panniculitic inflammation, often tracking systemic inflammatory burden.
- **Primary sclerosing cholangitis**: gut–liver axis, aberrant lymphocyte homing, bile duct immune injury; strongly linked to UC.
- **Venous thrombosis / PE/DVT**: inflammation-induced hypercoagulability via cytokine-driven endothelial activation, platelet activation, tissue factor, and impaired fibrinolysis.

Caveat: these are EHR diagnosis-code frequencies, not adjudicated prevalence; broad concepts like “arthritis” may include nonspecific or degenerative disease, so the more specific spondyloarthritis/uveitis/PSC/skin diagnoses are more biologically interpretable.
