| PTSD | 3,079 | Insomnia 13.54%, headache 12.47%, nausea 7.53% |
| OCD | 1,702 | Insomnia 8.70%, headache 8.05%, nausea 4.11% |
| Premenstrual dysphoric disorder | 431 | Headache 10.67%, insomnia 7.19%, nausea 3.94% |

Overall, **insomnia, headache, and nausea** are the most common SSRI-associated side-effect diagnoses across nearly all treatment indications. **Diarrhea, nausea/vomiting, and dizziness** form the next tier. Tremor, erectile dysfunction, somnolence, and hyperhidrosis/sweating were less common, generally around or below ~1%.

Caveat: these are diagnosis-code co-occurrences after SSRI initiation, not adjudicated adverse drug reactions, so confounding by indication and baseline symptom burden may inflate counts.
