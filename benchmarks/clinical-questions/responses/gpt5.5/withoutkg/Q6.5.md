5. **Opioid use disorder**

6. **Sedative/hypnotic/anxiolytic use disorder**

Bottom line: the shared biology most plausibly converges on **mesolimbic dopamine reward circuitry**, with substance-specific modulation through **GABA–glutamate balance**, **endocannabinoid**, **nicotinic acetylcholine**, **opioid**, and **monoaminergic norepinephrine/serotonin** systems.
