## Agent's Response

Using OMOP EHR data, adult heart-failure inpatient hospitalizations **declined substantially** across the available two-year window, while 30-day readmission rates were mostly stable in the low-40% range before falling in late 2025.

Key results:

| Period | HF hospitalizations | 30-day readmissions | Readmission rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Aug–Oct 2024 | 3,661 | 1,544 | 42.17% |
| Sep–Nov 2025 | 697 | 260 | 37.30% |

Monthly pattern:
- Hospitalizations were about **1,200–1,400/month** in late 2024.
- They declined through 2025 to **347 in Sep**, **227 in Oct**, and **123 in Nov 2025**.
- Readmission rates were generally **~40–45%** through Aug 2025, then lower in Sep–Nov 2025.
- Dec 2025 had only 31 hospitalizations and a 19.35% readmission rate, but should be treated as incomplete because 30-day follow-up is not fully observable.

Caveat: although the request asks for the last two years as of July 2026, this query found HF inpatient data only through **2025-12-23**, so 2026 months could not be meaningfully assessed.
