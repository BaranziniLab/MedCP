## Agent's Response

Across 2023–2025, monthly CDI diagnoses were roughly stable in 2023–2024, then dropped sharply during 2025:

- **CDI diagnoses**
  - 2023: **3,812 total**, mean **318/month**
  - 2024: **3,852 total**, mean **321/month**
  - 2025: **1,972 total**, mean **164/month**
- **Inpatient broad-spectrum antibiotic starts**
  - 2023: **196,461 total**, mean **16,372/month**
  - 2024: **223,116 total**, mean **18,593/month**
  - 2025: **116,499 total**, mean **9,708/month**

Same-month CDI vs broad-spectrum starts showed:

- **All 36 months:** Pearson **r ≈ 0.89**
- **2023–2024 only:** Pearson **r ≈ 0.16**
- **CDI vs prior-month antibiotic starts, all months:** Pearson **r ≈ 0.87**

Interpretation: the strong full-window association is mostly driven by both series falling together in 2025, especially late 2025, which may reflect incomplete data capture. In the more stable 2023–2024 period, higher CDI months did **not** clearly coincide with higher inpatient broad-spectrum antibiotic-start months. This is month-level ecological evidence only, not patient-level causal timing.
