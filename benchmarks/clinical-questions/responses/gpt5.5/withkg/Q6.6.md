The MedCP knowledge graph supports a shared immune-axis explanation: IBD and common inflammatory comorbidities share genes/pathways involving cytokine–cytokine receptor signaling, T-cell receptor/activation programs, TNF/NF-κB signaling, JAK/STAT/STAT3 signaling, and the IL-23/IL-17 axis. Interpretation: the frequent arthritis/spondyloarthritis, psoriasis, and uveitis pattern is consistent with systemic mucosal–barrier immune dysregulation, Th17/IL-23 signaling, TNF-driven inflammation, antigen presentation via HLA loci, and JAK/STAT cytokine propagation. PSC likely reflects gut–liver immune trafficking and shared epithelial/innate immune mechanisms; thromboembolism may reflect systemic inflammation–driven hypercoagulability rather than a single shared autoimmune pathway.
