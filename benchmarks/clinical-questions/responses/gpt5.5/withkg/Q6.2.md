## Agent's Response

Among patients with T2DM and at least two valid HbA1c values in the first year after diagnosis, mean HbA1c fell from **7.94% to 7.11%**, an average reduction of **0.83 HbA1c percentage points** over ~229 days, equivalent to **1.85 percentage points/year**.

By first diabetes drug recorded in the first year:

| First drug class | n | Baseline HbA1c | Last HbA1c in year 1 | Mean reduction | Annualized reduction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No diabetes drug recorded | 4,190 | 7.31 | 6.87 | 0.44 | 0.94 / yr |
| Insulin | 3,539 | 8.95 | 7.45 | 1.50 | 3.49 / yr |
| Biguanide / metformin | 2,131 | 7.63 | 6.97 | 0.66 | 1.38 / yr |
| Sulfonylurea | 369 | 8.06 | 7.59 | 0.46 | 0.95 / yr |
| SGLT2 inhibitor | 177 | 7.26 | 6.93 | 0.32 | 0.70 / yr |
| GLP-1 analogue | 132 | 7.26 | 6.69 | 0.57 | 1.26 / yr |
| DPP-4 inhibitor | 112 | 7.59 | 7.40 | 0.18 | 0.39 / yr |
| Combination oral agent | 63 | 7.60 | 7.44 | 0.16 | 0.69 / yr |
| Other glucose-lowering drug | 46 | 7.02 | 6.77 | 0.24 | 0.46 / yr |
| Thiazolidinedione | 31 | 7.25 | 7.29 | -0.04 | -0.28 / yr |

Interpretation: the largest apparent HbA1c reduction was in patients whose first recorded drug was **insulin**, but they also started with much higher HbA1c, so this is likely confounded by indication/severity. **Metformin/biguanide** starters had a moderate mean reduction. Patients with no recorded diabetes drug still improved, possibly reflecting lifestyle treatment, outside prescriptions, missing medication capture, or regression to the mean.

Cohort definition used: first `condition_era` for standard T2DM concept `201826`; HbA1c LOINC concepts `3004410`/`3005673`, values 3–20%; baseline = first HbA1c within 90 days after diagnosis; follow-up = last HbA1c after baseline and within 365 days; first drug = first ATC A10 diabetes drug exposure within first year.
