## Agent's Response

Monthly incident lung-cancer diagnoses were **fairly stable in 2023–2024**, then show a **sharp decline in mid-to-late 2025**, which is likely influenced by incomplete/censored recent data.

- **Case definition:** first recorded lung-cancer diagnosis per patient, using OMOP descendants of lung malignant neoplasm concepts.

- **2024:** 1,228 incident diagnoses; mean **102/month** — essentially flat vs 2023.
- **2025:** 716 incident diagnoses; mean **60/month**, but the drop is concentrated after May:
  - Jan–May 2025: ~**96/month**

Notable peaks were **Mar 2023: 124**, **May 2024: 120**, and **Jan 2025: 116**. The apparent decline after mid-2025 should be interpreted cautiously as likely reflecting data-lag or incomplete capture rather than a true epidemiologic decrease.
