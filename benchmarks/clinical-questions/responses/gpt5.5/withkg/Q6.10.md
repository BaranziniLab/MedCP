Biologic explanation from the KG + clinical data:
- The KG links sickle cell disease/anemia to **HBB** and hemoglobin genes; HBB participates in **erythrocyte homeostasis**, **oxygen transport**, **inflammatory response**, **reactive oxygen species metabolism**, and **response to oxidative stress**.
- Clinically, SCD patients also had splenic dysfunction diagnoses: **functional asplenia** in 63 patients, **acute sickle splenic sequestration crisis** in 59, **hyposplenism** in 37, and **asplenia** in 27.
- Mechanistically, chronic sickling/hemolysis → vaso-occlusion and splenic infarction/autosplenectomy → impaired splenic macrophage filtering and weaker opsonophagocytic clearance, especially for encapsulated bacteria.
- Hemolysis and ischemia-reperfusion promote oxidative stress, endothelial activation, complement/inflammatory activation, and neutrophil/monocyte dysfunction, which can impair effective pathogen clearance while increasing inflammatory complications.
- This fits the observed pattern: many **respiratory viral/upper-airway infections**, **pneumonia**, **UTI**, and clinically severe bacterial/inflammatory syndromes such as **septic shock**.
