- Liver-test elevation: ALT/AST/alk phos/bilirubin above local `range_high`, or explicit “above reference range” measurement concepts.
- Window: index date through `<180` days after first biologic era in that class.

Caveat: these are crude observed EHR rates, not adjusted comparative risks; class differences may reflect confounding by indication, prior therapy, monitoring intensity, and incomplete event capture.
