Biologic rationale:

- **TNF inhibitors** directly suppress a central inflammatory effector pathway. TNF is tied to inflammatory stress responses, cell death, immune activation, and tissue injury; blocking it can rapidly dampen acute colitis biology.
- **Gut-selective integrin biology** targets leukocyte trafficking rather than the inflammatory cytokine cascade itself. The KG links:
  - `ITGB7` to immune response in gut-associated lymphoid tissue, leukocyte adhesion, tethering/rolling.
  - `MADCAM1` to leukocyte migration, cellular extravasation, and mononuclear-cell migration.
  - `ITGA4` to leukocyte/cell motility.
- Thus integrin blockade is mechanistically elegant and gut-selective, but in severe UC it is often slower-onset because it prevents new lymphocyte recruitment rather than immediately neutralizing ongoing TNF-driven inflammation.

So: **lower early steroid-rescue burden favors TNF inhibitors**, while **integrin blockade is supported by α4β7/MAdCAM-1 gut-homing biology but may be less effective for rapid 60-day rescue in severe disease.**
