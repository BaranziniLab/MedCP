## Mineral metabolism mechanisms (CKD–MBD) that could explain the links

As GFR falls, **phosphate retention, low calcitriol, secondary hyperparathyroidism, and rising FGF-23/Klotho deficiency** form a coupled axis that drives cardiovascular disease:

- **Hyperphosphatemia + Ca × P product → vascular/valvular calcification.** High phosphate transforms vascular smooth-muscle cells into an osteoblast-like phenotype (via the Pit-1 transporter, Runx2/BMP-2 signaling), producing medial (Mönckeberg) arterial calcification and aortic-valve calcification — mechanistically linking to the arterial stiffness, PVD, IHD, and valvular disease seen above.
- **Arterial stiffening → hypertension, LVH, heart failure.** Medial calcification raises pulse-wave velocity and pulse pressure, increasing afterload; this promotes LV hypertrophy and the high heart-failure prevalence.
- **FGF-23 elevation → LV hypertrophy.** FGF-23 rises early to defend against phosphate load; at high levels it directly induces (Klotho-independent) LVH via FGFR4/calcineurin-NFAT signaling — a leading explanation for the heart-failure/LVH burden.
- **Secondary hyperparathyroidism.** Elevated PTH is associated with myocardial fibrosis, LVH, and possibly arrhythmia substrate (contributing to the atrial-fibrillation signal).
- **Calcitriol/vitamin-D deficiency.** Loss of active vitamin D removes a suppressor of the renin-angiotensin system (favoring hypertension) and of vascular calcification, and is linked to endothelial dysfunction.
- **Klotho deficiency.** Reduced soluble Klotho removes a vasculo-protective, anti-calcification factor, amplifying all of the above.

**Caveats:** these counts reflect co-occurring diagnoses in this OMOP dataset (lifetime condition records, not incident events at a fixed CKD stage), so they measure comorbidity *prevalence*, not causal effect. The mechanisms above are the established CKD–MBD pathophysiology from the literature; this EHR query establishes the association pattern, not the mechanism itself. Hypertension is also frequently the *cause* of CKD rather than a downstream consequence, so its dominance is bidirectional.
