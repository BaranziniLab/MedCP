**How this was defined**
- **New start**: first-ever oral isotretinoin exposure (capsule/tablet/oral descendants of ingredient `984232`); topical/vaginal forms excluded.
- **Adolescent**: age 10–19 at the start date.
- **ALT**: quantitative serum/plasma ALT (LOINC concept `3006923`), any result 0–90 days after start; the max per patient was used.
- **> 2× ULN**: values are in IU/L (≈ U/L) and the lab's own `range_high` was **not populated** in this dataset, so I applied a **fixed ULN of 40 IU/L → threshold 80 IU/L**. Only ~47% (845/1,800) had any ALT measured in the window.

**Caveats**
- The result is sensitive to the ULN assumption. Pediatric sex-specific ULNs are lower (~22–30 U/L for girls/boys), which would raise the count; using 80 IU/L is the conservative adult-based threshold.
