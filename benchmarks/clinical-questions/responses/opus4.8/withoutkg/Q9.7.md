## Candidate mechanism

- **Nintedanib** is an intracellular triple angiokinase inhibitor (VEGFR, FGFR, PDGFR). GI toxicity — diarrhea prominently — is a recognized class effect of tyrosine-kinase inhibitors, plausibly via interference with VEGFR/PDGFR-dependent intestinal epithelial and microvascular homeostasis. Its GI effects (nausea > diarrhea) and photosensitivity are the other characteristic tolerability issues.
