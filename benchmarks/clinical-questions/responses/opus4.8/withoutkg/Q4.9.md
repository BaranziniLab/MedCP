- **2019:** ~1,253/month

**Key observations:**

- **Mild recovery mid-2020**, then a return to a gently declining baseline through 2021.
- **Lowest month:** Aug 2021 (1,062). **Highest month:** Mar 2019 (1,359).
- **No strong seasonality** — fluctuations are within a fairly narrow ±10% band with no consistent monthly pattern.

**Important caveats:**
- "New" = each patient's *first-ever recorded* depressive-disorder diagnosis in this database, so counts are sensitive to when patients entered the system (left-censoring can inflate early-period counts).
- Values track *diagnosed/recorded* depression, driven by healthcare utilization — not underlying community incidence. The early-2020 dip is most plausibly an access/measurement artifact of the pandemic, not a decline in disease.
