## Most common SSRI-associated side effects in the cohort

Across ~63k SSRI-exposed patients (escitalopram and sertraline dominate exposure), the side-effect ranking is strikingly stable across indications. The **top three everywhere are insomnia, constipation, and headache**, followed by the GI cluster (nausea, diarrhea).

**Key patterns:**
- **Insomnia** is #1 in every indication (~20% in depression/anxiety/OCD).
- **Panic disorder** patients report every side effect at markedly higher rates — insomnia 32.9%, headache 24.8%, nausea 22.4% — likely reflecting greater somatic symptom sensitivity/reporting in this group rather than a true pharmacologic difference.
- **Sexual side effects** (erectile dysfunction ~2.7%) and **sweating** (<1%) are almost certainly under-captured — these are poorly coded as structured diagnoses in EHRs.

### Important caveats
- These are **conditions recorded on/after first SSRI exposure**, not adjudicated adverse drug reactions. Many (insomnia, headache, nausea) overlap with the underlying psychiatric illness or unrelated causes — this is association, not attributable causality.
- No comparison/unexposed group or baseline-symptom subtraction was applied, so these are crude cumulative prevalences.
- Indication groups overlap (a patient can appear under both depression and anxiety).
