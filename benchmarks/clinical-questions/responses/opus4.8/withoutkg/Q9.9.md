**Reading the numbers**
- VTE codes were ~1.8× more frequent in the progestin-only group (1.46% vs 0.80%), and migraine codes were somewhat higher in the COC group (8.51% vs 6.85%).
- The higher VTE rate in progestin-only users runs opposite to the expected pharmacology (estrogen is the pro-thrombotic component). This is almost certainly **confounding by indication**, not a protective COC effect — see caveats.

**Important caveats (this is an observational code-count, not an adjusted effect estimate)**
- **Progestin-only group is heterogeneous and higher-risk.** It captures descendants of all progestins, so it includes DMPA/LNG-IUS/implant *and* oral medroxyprogesterone/norethindrone used for HRT, abnormal uterine bleeding, and endometriosis, plus LNG 1.5 mg emergency contraception. - **No baseline/washout exclusion.** Prevalent VTE and migraine at index are not removed, so these are coded-occurrence rates, not incident rates.
- **No adjustment** for age, BMI, smoking, cancer, surgery, pregnancy — all strong VTE confounders.
- **Codes ≠ events**: reflects documentation, and migraine especially is chronic/prevalent so its "12-month" rate largely reflects ongoing coding, not new onset.
