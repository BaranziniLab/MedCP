From the OMOP EHR cohort (first regimen started within 90 days of first RA diagnosis; flare proxy = glucocorticoid burst or RA-related inpatient/ED visit in the 90 days after regimen start):

Absolute difference ≈ **4.9 percentage points** favoring biologic + MTX. Note the effect is modest and this is observational (confounding by indication is likely — sicker/seropositive patients are preferentially escalated to biologics, which would bias *against* the biologic arm, so the true efficacy gap may be larger). ## Immune-pathway differences that plausibly explain it

The two strategies act at different points of the RA inflammatory cascade, which is why targeted cytokine blockade tends to suppress early flares more reliably:

- **Biologic + MTX — targeted cytokine/costimulation blockade.** Biologics directly neutralize the dominant effector node. The KG confirms these effectors are up-regulated/associated in RA:
  - **TNF** (adalimumab, etanercept, infliximab, golimumab, certolizumab) — the apical driver of synovial inflammation.
  - **IL6 / IL6R** (tocilizumab, sarilumab) — drives acute-phase response, so blocking it strongly damps the CRP/ESR-based flare signal.
  - **CTLA4 costimulation** (abatacept) — blocks the CD80/86–CD28 T-cell activation step.
  - **CD20 / MS4A1** (rituximab) — depletes autoreactive B cells (consistent with the elevated B-cell markers **CD19, CD20, CD79A, MZB1** seen in the KG's RA-increased proteins).
  MTX adds an anti-proliferative/adenosine-mediated anti-inflammatory effect and reduces anti-drug antibodies, sustaining biologic levels.

- **Triple conventional DMARDs — broad, indirect immunomodulation.** MTX (adenosine/folate), sulfasalazine (NF-κB/prostaglandin), and hydroxychloroquine (lysosomal/TLR–antigen-presentation) act diffusely and slowly, with no single dominant cytokine target. Onset is slower and ceiling of suppression lower, so more residual **TNF/IL-6-driven** activity persists in the first 90 days → higher early-flare proportion.

The genes the KG associates with RA — **TNF, IL6, IL6R, IL1B, IL17A, JAK1, TYK2, STAT3, CTLA4, HLA-DRB1, PTPN22, TNFAIP3, IL2RA** — map onto exactly the pathways biologics hit directly (TNF, IL-6/JAK-STAT, T-/B-cell costimulation) but conventional DMARDs only modulate indirectly.
