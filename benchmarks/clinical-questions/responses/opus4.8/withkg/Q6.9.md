- **Leptin–melanocortin (POMC → MC4R) signaling.** Central regulator of satiety; leptin resistance in obesity co-tracks with depressive symptoms ("leptin resistance" hypothesis of depression). MC4R also modulates anxiety-like behavior.
- **Serotonin (5-HT) & dopamine reward.** 5-HT links satiety with mood (shared target of SSRIs); dopaminergic reward/reward-deficiency ties food-seeking to ADHD and to binge/eating disorders.
- **Ghrelin & NPY/AgRP (orexigenic).** Ghrelin rises with stress and stimulates appetite while also being anxiolytic/antidepressant acutely — a candidate node coupling stress, mood, and feeding.
- **Neuroinflammation (IL-6, TNF-α, CRP).** Adipose-derived inflammation is associated with both depression and impaired satiety signaling — a shared biological substrate.
- **BDNF.** Low BDNF is implicated in both energy balance/satiety and depression/anxiety; BDNF–TrkB is downstream of MC4R.
- **Iatrogenic loop.** Second-generation antipsychotics and some mood/ADHD-adjacent medications drive weight gain (H1/5-HT2C antagonism), a non-biological but clinically major link.
