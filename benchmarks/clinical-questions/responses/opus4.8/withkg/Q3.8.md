Among new trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole (TMP-SMX) users, hyperkalemia (serum/plasma/blood potassium > 5.0 mmol/L) is recorded within 30 days in about **7.6%** of patients.

- **New users** (first-ever TMP-SMX exposure): 105,546
- **With ≥1 potassium > 5.0 mmol/L in the 30 days after first exposure:** 8,041
- **Incidence:** 8,041 / 105,546 = **7.62%**
