- **Pravastatin** (hydrophilic, no CYP3A4 metabolism) and **fluvastatin** (lowest affinity for muscle HMG-CoA reductase among the older agents) are the next-lowest risk profiles. Both show fewer 30-day CK >10× ULN reports than atorvastatin or simvastatin in claims-based analyses.

- Among the lipophilic agents, **rosuvastatin** sits between pravastatin and atorvastatin/simvastatin for CK elevation — it is hydrophilic like pravastatin but carries a stronger dose-dependent myopathy signal at higher doses (20–40 mg), comparable to atorvastatin 40 mg.

- **Atorvastatin** and **simvastatin** (both lipophilic + major CYP3A4 metabolism) carry the highest short-term myopathy signals. Simvastatin has dose-dependent risk that spikes markedly above 40 mg in FDA warnings.

**Rank order, lowest to highest CK/myopathy signal:**
1. Pitavastatin ≈ Pravastatin (lowest)
2. Fluvastatin 
3. Rosuvastatin
4. Atorvastatin
5. Simvastatin (highest)
