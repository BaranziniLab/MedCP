## Do IL-5/IL-5R biologics achieve greater 90-day eosinophil suppression than anti-IgE?

**Yes.** The depth and mechanism of eosinophil suppression differ fundamentally:

### Target-level difference
- **Mepolizumab / reslizumab** bind free **IL-5 cytokine**, preventing it from engaging **IL-5Rα** on the surface of eosinophils. This removes a key survival signal.
- **Benralizumab** also blocks IL-5 signaling (by binding **IL-5Rα**), but carries an additional mechanism: its humanized IgG1 Fc domain induces **antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC)** against IL-5Rα⁺ eosinophils, driving rapid and near-complete depletion.
- **Omalizumab** binds free **IgE**, preventing it from engaging **FcεRI** on mast cells and basophils — but IgE signaling does *not* directly target eosinophils.

### Mechanistic consequences by day 90

| Dimension | IL-5/IL-5R biologics (mepolizumab / reslizumab / benralizumab) | Anti-IgE (omalizumab) |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct eosinophil suppression** | Direct — blocks eosinophil-specific cytokine signaling and survival, or kills them via ADCC (benralizumab). | Indirect only — reduces mast-cell–derived Th2 cytokine drive; no direct anti-eosinophil effect. |
| **Blood eosinophil reduction at 90 d** | ~70–95% decline (benralizumab often near-complete, especially with ADCC clearance). | ~30–50% decline on average (substantial but shallow compared with IL-5 blockade). |
| **Airway/lung tissue eosinophils** | Robust depletion; eosinophilic airway inflammation is markedly reduced. | Minimal direct effect on tissue eosinophils; some indirect reduction via lowered Th2 drive. |
| **Additional immune effects** | Lowers serum IgE indirectly (IL-5 promotes B-cell class switching to IgE); suppresses BALT germinal centers. | Lowers free serum IgE ~60–80%, but does *not* reduce IL-5 production; downstream eosinophil activation persists if IL-5 is still present from other sources (e.g., epithelial cells, Th1/2 cells). |

### Bottom line
Both classes are approved for severe asthma and both reduce exacerbation rates versus maintenance ICS/LABA monotherapy. But **IL-5/IL-5R-targeting agents achieve deeper and more sustained eosinophil suppression at 90 days** because they interrupt the *eosinophil-specific* branch of type-2 immunity directly, whereas omalizumab suppresses only the *IgE-driven mast-cell/basophil branch*. Benralizumab is especially potent due to the dual mechanism: receptor blockade + ADCC-mediated killing.
