**Female-predominant differential:**
- **Osteoporosis** (59.9\% F vs <1\% M) and **glaucoma** (61.1\%\* F) reflect sex-dimorphic endocrine trajectories: postmenopausal estrogen withdrawal accelerates bone resorption and alters trabecular microarchitecture; estrogen also supports retinal ganglion-cell survival via ERα signaling in the visual pathway (~20-fold higher prevalence ratio in women).
- **Depression** and **anxiety** (62–70\% F vs 48–64\% M): plausible mechanisms include sex-hormone interaction with serotonergic neurotransmission, microglial priming by IFN-γ signaling, and the compounding effect of caregiver burden in elder women.
- **Osteoarthritis**: higher prevalence driven by joint-laxity changes post-menopause; bidirectional with dementia via shared inflammatory mediators (IL-6, TNF-α) that disrupt hippocampal neurogenesis.

**Male-predominant differential:**
- **Hypertension & CKD (~52\% vs 47–48\%)**: male cardiometabolic phenotype (earlier visceral adiposity, less lipid-spillover to subcutaneous/pelvic depots) accelerates cerebral small-vessel disease, a known driver of vascular dementia subtype and a comorbidity cluster that feeds back into global cognitive decline via amyloid-β clearance impairment at the blood–brain barrier.
- **Urinary incontinence** (49\% M vs 42\% F): in men with dementia this is frequently secondary to BPH/lUTI rather than stress continence loss; prostatic hypertrophy itself up-regulates systemic IL-6 which has been linked to greater tau burden.

---

**Biological synthesis:**
1. **Sex-hormone–neuroinflammation axis**: estrogen confers neuroprotection via ERβ-mediated anti-inflammatory signaling and Aβ clearance; its loss widens the depression/anxiety/comorbidity gap in women.
2. **Vascular vs inflammatory phenotypes**: men present with earlier cardiovascular end-organ damage (HTN, CKD), which accelerates mixed/vascular dementia; women's comorbidity cluster is dominated by musculoskeletal + affective symptoms but their overall dementia rate is higher (~1.4:1 F:M in this cohort).
3. **Microglial X-chromosome dosage**: CXCR3 and other X-linked immune genes give female microglia an amplified inflammatory response, which may explain the higher depression/anxiety comorbidity burden even after adjusting for age.
