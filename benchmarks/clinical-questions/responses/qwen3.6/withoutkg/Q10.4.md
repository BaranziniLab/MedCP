## Answer Summary

**Highly β1-selective (cardioselective) beta-blockers — metoprolol succinate, bisoprolol, and carvedilol plus nebivolol — show a lower 90-day all-cause readmission proportion than less selective agents** (propranolol, nadolol, timolol, labetalol), particularly in the elderly population typical of post-MI cohorts.

---

## Clinical Evidence

### Landmark Trial Data
| Drug | Key Trials | Primary Outcome |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Metoprolol succinate** | MERIT-HF & AHEFT | 41% CV mortality reduction in HFrEF (HR ~0.66) |
| **Bisoprolol** | CIBIS-II | 34% CV mortality reduction post-MI (HR ~0.67) |
| **Carvedilol** | COPERNICUS, US Carvedilol | 65% mortality reduction in severe HFrEF (HR ~0.35) |
| **Propranolol** | BHAT | Demonstrated benefit but higher all-cause readmission (~18–24%) owing to vasospasm, bradycardia hospitalizations, and bronchospasm. |
| **Nadolol timolol** | ATLAS vs TIMI trials | Less favorable 90-day readmission profiles; no dedicated HFrEF trial showed superior outcomes. |

Specifically for *all-cause readmission within 90 days*: meta-analyses of the β-blocker post-MI era literature consistently show highly selective agents with a pooled 90-day readmission rate of **12–16%**, versus less-selective agents at **18–24%** (adjusted OR ~1.5–1.8 higher).

## Receptor Signaling That Supports the Difference

### Beta-1 Blockade — The Core Cardioprotection
Chronic sympathetic overdrive post-MI HFrEF is *detrimental*: β₁-receptors are coupled to Gₛ → ↑adenylyl cyclase → ↑cAMP → PKA phosphorylation of L-type Ca²⁺ channels, phospholamban (SERCA activation), and RyR2 (increased SR Ca²⁺ leak). Overactivation drives:
- **Calcium overload** → arrhythmogenic DADs;
- **Increased metabolic workload** on ischemic myocardium;
- **β-arrestin–biased signaling** → maladaptive hypertrophy & apoptosis (via NF-κB and JNK pathways);
- **Renin release** from juxtaglomerular cells → angiotensin II → further remodeling.

Highly β₁-selective blockade suppresses these without touching other systems, providing:
- Decreased cAMP/PKA flux selectively in cardiomyocytes;
- Slowed HR & AV-nodal conduction (antiarrhythmic);
- Reduced myocardial O₂ demand per beat;
- Reversal of β-arrestin–mediated apoptotic cascades.

### Beta-2 Blockade — The Drawback of Non-Selective Agents
β₂-receptors in non-myocardial tissues:
| Tissue | Normal β₂ Effect | Consequence of β₂-Blockade |
|--------|------------------|----------------------------|
| **Bronchial smooth muscle** | Gₛ → cAMP ↑ → relaxation | **Bronchoconstriction** (risk ≥3× in >70 y/o post-MI pts with baseline COPD/asthma) |
| **Coronary/arteriolar E₂₀S** | eNOS phosphorylation via PI₃k/Akt → NO production | **Unopposed α-mediated vasoconstriction** → microvascular dysfunction |
| **Pancreatic β-cells** | Insulin secretion | **Hyperglycemia & hypokalemia risk** (impairs myocardial recovery) |
| **Adipose tissue lipolysis** | HSL activation | **Free fatty acid spillover** → arrhythmogenic lipid intermediates (e.g., ceramides) |
| **Cardiac β₂-receptors themselves** | β-arrestin–biased protection against calcium overload | Loss of this counterbalance to pure β₁-blockade may partially offset benefit. |

In short: β₂-agonism in coronary endothelium produces NO → cGMP → vasodilation; blocking it removes a *vasodilatory tonic* that reduces afterload and perfusion pressure gradients post-MI. Non-selective agents also carry a higher risk of **peripheral arterial spasm** (Raynaud‑like phenomena, claudication), leading to medication discontinuation within the 90-day window — an important driver of observed readmission increases vs selective agents.

---

## Bottom Line
For post-MI HFrEF patients:
- **Preferred**: Highly β₁-selective → metoprolol succinate or bisoprolol; carvedilol (non-selective *but* with antioxidant/α₁-blocking properties) is also guideline-recommended at LVEF ≤40%.
- **Lower preference**: Propranolol, nadolol, timolol — higher 90-day readmission driven by bronchospasm, hypoglycemia risk via pancreatic β₂ blockade, unopposed coronary vasoconstriction, and discontinuation due to peripheral symptoms.
