### Shared immune signaling underlying EIM pathogenesis

The unifying mechanistic framework converges on three interconnected axes:

**1. Th17 / IL-23 axis — the central shared pathway.**

* Both gut mucosa and EIM (peripheral arthritis, psoriasis-like skin lesions, uveitis) tissue demonstrate **IL-17A/F**-producing T-cells driven by **IL-23 + IL-1β**.
* STAT3-RORγt transcriptional program is shared.
* Explains clinical efficacy: anti-TNF (infliximab/adalimumab ± ustekinumab) often improves peripheral arthritis and skin EIM, and vedolizumab's gut-selectivity can leave some EIMs untreated because non-α4β7 homing T-cells drive extraintestinal sites.

**2. TNF-α / NF-κB axis — the primary systemic effector.**

* Elevated TNF-α in IBD is not just a mucosa-restricted cytokine; serum/circulating TNF-α levels correlate with EIM activity (uoric arthritis flares, acute phase reactant CRP).
* In axial disease, IL-23/Th17 remains dominant alongside TNF-α but with more synovial/mesenchymal involvement.

**3. Mucosal-homing / trafficking — the gatekeepers of organ specificity.**

* **VLA-4 (CD49d/β1 integrin), α4β7**, and **CCR9** direct effector T-cells toward gut mucosa during inflammation; their blockade with vedolizumab is why EIMs are sometimes incompletely controlled.
* CXCL10/CXCR3 axis links peripheral immune cells to skin/liver sites, particularly in PSC.

**Why not every drug treats all EIMs:** the key clinical lesson is that gut-homing blockade (vedolizumab) spares some EIMs because they are driven by non-gut-homing T-cells or local innate lymphocytes (e.g., liver ILC3/NK cells in PSC), whereas systemic cytokine blockade (anti-TNF, anti-IL-23p19) penetrates more broadly.

### Clinical takeaway: which EIMs track with gut vs. are independent

* **Gut-dependent EIMs** (parallel disease activity): episcleritis, erythema nodosum, peripheral arthritis (Type 1 PAU), aphthous ulcers.
* **Gut-independent EIMs** (can persist or flare during mucosal remission): axial arthropathy/spondyloarthritis, pyoderma gangrenosum (sometimes), PSC (progresses regardless of IBD activity).
